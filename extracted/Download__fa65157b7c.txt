Subject matter:

Exchange of views with representatives of the "One System
Approach" coalition on energy efficiency, electrification and
competitiveness. Discussion on the implementation of the current
policy framework and reflections on the post-2030 framework.

Main points
raised and
positions
expressed:

•
•
•
•

Industry highlighted the importance of regulatory certainty,
long-term investment signals and effective implementation of
existing legislation.
Views were exchanged on the contribution of energy
efficiency and electrification to competitiveness and industrial
investment.
Participants discussed elements of the post-2030 framework,
including future policy direction and implementation
challenges.
The Commission underlined the importance of
implementation, continued stakeholder engagement and
maintaining dialogue on future policy developments.
