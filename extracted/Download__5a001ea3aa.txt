Conclusions:

• Serentschy Advisory Services presented precision timing as a strategic and geopolitical
capability, using the Arctic as a case study.
• Commission representatives outlined existing EU support for quantum-clock research
and industrialisation.
• The discussion covered technological maturity, future integration with European space
and PNT systems, the international standardisation process, and cooperation with likeminded countries.
The Commission will further assess the state of play on quantum-clock standardisation
and relevant European and international participation.
