Main points

The participants discussed the implementation of the Cyber
Resilience Act (CRA), and particularly the requirement to
provide security updates free of charge, unless otherwise
agreed between a manufacturer and a business user in
relation to a tailor-made product with digital elements.
The Commission took note and proposed to continue the
exchange at technical level.
