Subject matter:

EU-US Energy Trade, EU Methane Regulation

Main points
raised and
positions
expressed:

Companies highlighted their commitment to the reduction of
methane emissions and welcomed the increased clarity the
Recommendations on the EU Methane Regulation provide. They
shared their concerns on insufficient clarity on compliance
pathways, alignment between Member States in implementation
and security of supply for Europe. ENER reaffirmed the
implementation of the EU Methane Regulation and the guidance
on delaying the implementation on penalties for the next three
years. It also underlined its commitment to work with Member
States on implementing the Regulations in a consistent manner
and ensuring granular guidance on compliance pathways.

Conclusions:

Continue exchanges between teams.
